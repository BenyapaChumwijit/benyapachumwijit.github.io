ABOUT US

The Faculty of Engineering Basketball Club, Khon Kaen University, was founded for students of the Faculty of Engineering who are interested in and passionate about basketball, giving them a place to play and train together for various competitions within the university.

Our club warmly welcomes everyone who wants to play basketball. We treat each other like family, and we're committed to growing and improving our basketball skills together.


GOALS & OBJECTIVE

Our club focuses on training and developing basketball skills to prepare members for various competitions within the university. Beyond that, we aim to build meaningful connections among our members, helping everyone find new friends who share a passion for basketball and creating a warm, family-like community. Our club not only helps members improve their basketball skills, but also serves as a space where members can support and advise one another academically.

Club facts at a glance:

  • Founded by a small group of students
  • Open to all skill levels
  • Practices held seven days a week
  • Competes in inter-university friendly tournaments
